Songs — not built yet

This page is step 4 of the development order: it comes after Practice works end to end, with spaced repetition and all five levels. The shared foundations are already in place, though.

What already exists

harmony.js exposes parseLab and segmentsToExercises: a .lab file already yields exercises in exactly the shape Practice uses, with the synchronisation offset applied to the timings. The Parser page is there to check annotations before building on them.

quiz-ui.js is already agnostic about the audio source: it takes an exercise, shows the fields for the level, and calls onReplay. The only difference between the two pages remains the exercise's audio field.

What is missing

  • js/youtube.js — the iframe API, playback of a [start, end] range, and continuous scrolling.
  • Manual calibration of the offset: play the first annotated chord and nudge it until it lines up.
  • The three game modes: isolated segment, continuous scrolling, and review of only the chords you got wrong.
  • The key of each song, entered by hand in the metadata — the .lab files do not carry it.

Corpus

data/songs/index.json is the song list, and it is empty on purpose. The Isophonics, McGill Billboard and RWC annotations each come with their own terms of use, to be checked and credited before redistributing them here: this repository ships none of them. The only .lab present, data/songs/sample.lab, is synthetic — written to exercise the awkward corners of the parser.
